Next-Generation Event Ticketing with Facial Verification

Combat ticket scalping, illicit reselling, and identity theft. Our two-stage entrance system validates digital QR tokens and verifies ticket ownership using HOG Feature Descriptors, K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO).

1. Secure QR Token

Cryptographically unpredictable tokens hashed using SHA-256 in the database. Valid for exactly one successful venue entrance.

📷

2. HOG Feature Extraction

Standardized 128×128 facial alignment with 9 gradient orientation bins across 8×8 cells, yielding an 8,100-dimensional descriptor vector.

⚡

3. PSO-Optimized KNN

Instance-based identity matching with Particle Swarm Optimization searching for optimal neighborhood parameter k and rejection threshold.

Atomic Single-Use Ticket Check-In

When a participant arrives at the turnstile, the venue scanner decodes the QR code and initiates a 3-minute verification window. The live camera matches the face against the ticket owner. Only upon verified identity match is the ticket consumed in an atomic database transaction.
